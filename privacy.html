Ridvise Privacy Policy

Effective September 9, 2026. Updated September 16, 2026.

Ridvise Publisher ("the tool") is a private, single-user application operated by Anthony Benyameen, the owner of the Ridvise social media accounts. It exists only to publish Ridvise's own original videos to Ridvise's own accounts on TikTok and YouTube. It has no other users.

YouTube API Services

Ridvise Publisher uses YouTube API Services to upload Ridvise's own videos to the Ridvise YouTube channel (youtube.com/@ridviseyt). By using this tool you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

Information we collect

YouTube: when the account owner authorizes the tool with their Google account, Google provides an OAuth access token and refresh token, plus the channel ID and title of the authorized YouTube channel. The tool also reads back the status of videos it uploaded (for example, whether processing succeeded).

TikTok: when the account owner authorizes the tool with TikTok, TikTok provides an access token and basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar).

The tool does not collect any information about other YouTube or TikTok users, and it does not read, display, download or analyze anyone else's content.

How it is used

Tokens are used solely to upload and publish Ridvise's own videos (title, description, tags, privacy status) to the authorized accounts and to confirm the upload finished processing. Channel and profile information is used only to confirm the correct account is connected.

Storage and sharing

TikTok tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. YouTube OAuth tokens are held by Composio (composio.dev), the integration service that makes the YouTube API calls on the tool's behalf. No data is sold or shared with anyone else, and no data is used for advertising.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke Ridvise Publisher's access to your Google account at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. TikTok access can be revoked in TikTok's app settings. Revoking access stops all use of the tokens. Any stored authorization data is deleted within 30 days of revocation or of a deletion request sent to the contact below.
